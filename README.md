# Warm intro to angel investors

Who this is for: founders who can ask someone who already knows both sides. How this was compiled: a forwardable ask, not a scraped list. Fundraising is super easy when the intro is specific. Live matches: [investor match](https://roundfunded.com/en/tools/investor-match).

Last updated: 2026-09-02

Live investor names, counts, and matches are only on https://roundfunded.com/en/tools/investor-match. This repo is criteria and methodology, not the database.

## Problem

"Anyone who invests" is not an intro request. Name the person. Write the blurb.

## How to ask

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Specific person | Vague asks get ignored |
| Forwardable blurb, 3 to 4 lines | They should not have to rewrite you |
| Why this angel, one sentence | Portfolio, sector, or a check they already wrote |
| Easy out | "No worry if not a fit" |
| You do not CC the angel | That is a cold email wearing a costume |

Do not invent names of people who will intro you.

## What this is not

- Not a scraped name list
- Not a fund and not an accelerator
- Not a claim of partnership with Y Combinator, Antler, Techstars, or 500 Global
